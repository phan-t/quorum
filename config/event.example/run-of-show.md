## Things that go wrong

- **Audio does not play on the Desktop.** A browser will not start audio
  without a click in that tab. The preflight row exists for this.
- **Somebody joins late.** They get Bench Credit rather than a zero. Nothing
  to do.
- **A message should not be read out.** The console shows the next one before
  the room sees it. Skip advances past it without putting it on screen.
